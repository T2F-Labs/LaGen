\subsection{Limitations and Threats to Validity}
\label{subsec:evaluation-limitations}

We acknowledge several limitations in our comparative evaluation that may affect the generalizability of our findings.

\textbf{Methodological Limitations:}

\begin{compactlist}
    \item \textbf{Prototype vs Production}: Symphony comparisons based on research prototype
    \item \textbf{Ecosystem Maturity}: Existing platforms benefit from mature extension ecosystems
    \item \textbf{User Adoption}: Long-term usability requires longitudinal studies
    \item \textbf{Domain Specificity}: Evaluation focused on general-purpose development tasks
\end{compactlist}

\textbf{Threats to Validity:}

\begin{alertbox}
\textbf{Validity Considerations}

Our evaluation acalidity:
\begin{compactlist}
    \item \textbf{Internal Validity}: Performance measurements conducted in controlled environments
    \item \textbf{External Validity}: Results may not generalize to all development contexts
    \item \textbf{Construct Validity}: Metrics chosen may not capture all relevant aspects
    \item \textbf{Conclusion Validity}: Statistical significance requires larger sample sizes
\end{compactlist}
\end{alertbox}

The comparative evaluation demonstrates Symphony's architectural innovations while acknowledging the need for continued research to validate these contributions in production environments.
