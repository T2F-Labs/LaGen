\section*{Harmony Board UI}
\addcontentsline{toc}{section}{Harmony Board UI}

\lettrine{T}{he Harmony Board UI} provides comprehensive real-time monitoring and debugging capabilities for AI-first development workflows, representing a breakthrough in visualization and control of complex multi-agent systems. Our research introduces novel interface paradigms that make AI orchestration transparent, debuggable, and optimizable while maintaining the performance required for production development environments.
