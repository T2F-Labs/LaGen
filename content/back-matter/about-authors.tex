\begin{description}[leftmargin=4cm,labelwidth=3.5cm,itemsep=1.5cm]

\item[\textbf{\textcolor{brandPrimary}{AMR MUHMAED FATHY}}] 
\textit{Lead Architecture \& AI Systems Design}

Amr is the principal architect behind Symphony's innovative AI-first development environment. With expertise in artificial intelligence, software architecture, and human-computer interaction, he led the design of Symphony's revolutionary microkernel architecture and intelligent orchestration system. His research focuses on the intersection of AI and software development tools, particularly in creating systems where human creativity and artificial intelligence collaborate seamlessly.

\item[\textbf{\textcolor{brandSecondary}{MOHAMED}}] 
\textit{Backend Infrastructure \& Performance Engineering}

Mohamed architected Symphony's high-performance backend infrastructure, including the Rust-based microkernel and the sophisticated IPC communication system. His expertise in systems programming and performance optimization ensures Symphony delivers the ultra-low latency required for seamless AI-human collaboration. He specializes in distributed systems, concurrent programming, and performance-critical software design.

\item[\textbf{\textcolor{brandAccent}{MAHMOUD}}] 
\textit{Frontend Development \& User Experience Design}

Mahmoud crafted Symphony's elegant and intuitive user interface, transforming complex AI orchestration into accessible visual workflows. His expertise in modern web technologies, user experience design, and accessibility ensures that Symphony's revolutionary capabilities are presented through an interface that feels natural and empowering to developers. He specializes in React architecture, design systems, and human-centered design.

\item[\textbf{\textcolor{brandTertiary}{MOSTAFA}}] 
\textit{Extension System \& Developer Tools}

Mostafa designed and implemented Symphony's extensible architecture, including the innovative dual-ensemble system that balances performance with safety. His work on the extension marketplace and developer tooling enables the community to expand Symphony's capabilities while maintaining system integrity. He specializes in plugin architectures, developer experience, and software ecosystem design.

\item[\textbf{\textcolor{brandPrimary}{SALMA}}] 
\textit{Documentation \& Quality Assurance}

Salma ensured Symphony meets the highest standards of quality and usability through comprehensive testing, documentation, and user research. Her meticulous approach to quality assurance and her ability to translate complex technical concepts into clear documentation has been instrumental in making Symphony accessible to developers worldwide. She specializes in technical writing, software testing, and user research methodologies.

\end{description}
