\section*{Overview}
\addcontentsline{toc}{section}{Overview}

Symphony's development approach emphasizes research-driven innovation, modular architecture, and community collaboration. Our development guide reflects the academic rigor required for advancing the field of AI-assisted software development while maintaining practical applicability for real-world deployment.

The development process integrates theoretical research with empirical validation, ensuring that architectural decisions contribute meaningfully to the broader software engineering research community. This appendix serves as both a practical guide for contributors and a methodological framework for similar research endeavors.
