\section*{Overview}
\addcontentsline{toc}{section}{Overview}

The configuration system is designed to support Symphony's research objectives by providing transparent, reproducible settings that can be validated and version-controlled alongside research artifacts. This approach enables systematic evaluation of configuration impacts on system performance and user experience.

Symphony's configuration architecture demonstrates novel approaches to managing complex system settings while maintaining the flexibility required for research experimentation and practical deployment.
